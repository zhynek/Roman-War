## Categories for future studies

Use multiple categories when the evidence supports them: imperial/royal capital,
fortified town, castle complex, port, river crossing, market center, mining town,
craft center, religious center, agricultural service town, frontier stronghold.
These are research tags, not an exhaustive historical typology or implemented
game classes.

Choose one city and one reference period before modeling. Two periods of the
same city can share a study's historical snapshots; a radically different
reconstruction can become a separately indexed study with an explicit relation
to its predecessor.

Add entries to [catalog.json](catalog.json), then run the workspace validator.
