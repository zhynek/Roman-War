# Yenikapı — Early Settlement 0.1.0

Explore an original, interpretive village set around 6000 BCE on Istanbul's
historic peninsula. This is not a recovered village plan or a population estimate.
The Evidence & limits panel explains direct archaeology, regional analogy and
authored details. Only the village is implemented. The medieval Constantinople
app and campaign are separate and their releases/saves remain unchanged.

WASD / arrows: move. Right mouse drag or Tab: look. F: walk/free flight.
Q/E: down/up in flight. Shift: faster. Wheel: flight speed (3–120 m/s).
0: aerial survey. 1–8: places/interiors. I: evidence. H: interface.
Escape: release mouse. Click buildings to inspect their interpretation and ID.

All nine interiors are accessible; six dwelling models, two stores and a working
room illustrate household life. Fields, an outdoor hearth and a stream landing
complete the walking circuit. Roofs, furnishing arrangements, crops, canoe hull,
paths and exact shoreline are interpretive. No later monuments or city defenses
have been transplanted into the Neolithic scene.

Save view / Restore view use a separate viewing bookmark in:
`~/Library/Application Support/Roman War Yenikapi Early Settlement/early_settlement_view.json`.
No campaign or medieval creative saves are read or altered. Free flight has no
collision; returning to walking finds safe ground. Far land is a scenic backdrop.

The universal Mac app is ad-hoc signed, not Developer ID notarized. If macOS asks,
use Finder's Open action. Apple Silicon rendering is tested; Intel performance
has not been measured. Original procedural artwork has no people, animated
crafts, sound, farming or water simulation. Models are neutral PBR GLBs; editable
source includes the Godot project, evidence ledger and authoring documentation.
