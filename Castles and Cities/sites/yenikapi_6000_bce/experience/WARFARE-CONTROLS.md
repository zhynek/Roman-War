# Village warfare — preparation and controls

This local 0.14.0 chapter belongs to **Yenikapı — Early Settlement**. It is
independent of the Roman campaign and its saves. Use **New village** or load your
existing village, then **Defend the village → Enable village warfare**. Adoption
is explicit; loading an older save does not enable warfare or recurring threats.

## Prepare in ordinary village life

1. Keep food, care and timber work staffed. Build the **watch shelter**, prepare
   wooden sets in the working room, and commission **wooden watch kits**. Training
   and kit repair each need a real adult; equipment is never granted by a battle.
2. Build useful screens through the normal paid project queue. The store screen
   costs **12 timber / 16 work**; the landing screen costs **10 timber / 16 work**.
   Both require the watch shelter. Their entrances stay open. Maintain the watch
   asset: below condition 50, the screens lose their prepared-position protection.
3. Save assembly, defended approach, important place, reserve and fallback
   positions. Holding or defending a maintained screen's inner stand reduces
   frontal incoming damage. Its ends and entrance remain vulnerable.
4. Request **2 or 4 additional home-watch residents** when you need a larger
   force. The panel lists the actual people and equipment available. Extra watch
   competes with construction, timber and reserve work; finish urgent projects
   first and release the extra watch afterward.
5. Choose **Enable recurring threats** when ready for continuing encounters.
   Warnings follow at least eight quiet seasons, with two preparation seasons
   before the encounter. Established friendly contacts can add a warning season.
   Further threats wait for recovery, sufficient food and available defenders.

Three objectives recur: occupation of the stores; taking supplies from the
landing and carrying them away; and holding the northern approach. Read the
warning and stakes. Enemy capability is only known through observation; the
assessment does not promise numerical odds.

## Choose how to fight

- **Command personally:** deploy while paused, then begin and give orders.
- **Delegate command:** defenders use legal defensive placements and issue the
  same orders you can. Hand off or take command back during a live battle.
- **Resolve quickly:** the same delegated simulation runs in fast batches.
  **Stop & pause** keeps a coherent, resumable battle. Giving an order takes
  personal command back. Quick resolution still waits for outcome acceptance.

**Practice on a copy** uses your current forces and preparation without changing
village supplies, experience, injuries or history. Practice cannot overwrite the
continuing village save.

## Battle controls

| Action | Control |
|---|---|
| Select | Click a group; drag a rectangle; Shift adds; Ctrl/Cmd+A selects all |
| Give the selected order | Right-click clear ground or an observed enemy |
| Move / targeted attack / advance while engaging | M / T / A |
| Hold / defend / withdraw / face | H / D / R / F |
| Formation width | Column / Spread buttons; narrow passages automatically file the group |
| Pause / resume | Space; orders remain usable while paused |
| Pace | Speed 1× / 2× button |
| Camera | 0 overhead; V close; wheel zoom; middle-drag pan; trackpad pan/pinch |

Read the group panel for morale, fatigue, equipment condition, watch skill,
leadership and local support. Selection, destinations and facing indicators use
the same positions as the figures. Camera gestures do not issue orders. Hold in
safe ground to rest; retreat before exhaustion or morale collapse when possible.

**Save battle** pauses and saves deployment, active or ended-but-unaccepted
combat. **Resume saved battle** restores it paused. Seasons, workforce, spending
and construction wait until the current battle is accepted. Preparation and
plans can also be saved before mobilization.

## After the encounter

Accept the report once, then open **Battle aftermath** from the ordinary village
interface whenever needed. It retains named participants, household effects,
protected or lost supplies, the ending reason, equipment wear and earned
experience. Household and asset panels also show current recovery.

Keep treatment enabled and food available. A dedicated treatment worker can care
for two residents each fed season; wounds need one treatment season and
incapacitation three. Patients rest at home and are unavailable for work. Release
extra watch to free labor. Existing household care relieves strain. Ordinary kit
repair consumes a prepared set, timber and a worker; it replaces missing kits
and restores pooled condition, including when the kit count is already full.

Residents whose group fought gain recorded experience; every three engagement
records add one watch-skill point, capped by the authored tactical skill limit. No
experience is granted merely for mobilizing or practicing. Combat incapacitation
is recoverable in this milestone. Natural aging and lifecycle history continue;
combat does not delete residents or households.


## Civic progression and the in-game guide

Open **How to play** in the illustrated village dock. Its eleven lessons cover
ordinary work, civic readiness, earned rank versus current **Town support**, paid
watch preparation, command modes and recovery. A separate setup note explains
explicit chapter adoption; browsing or following a lesson link issues no order.
Town remains the highest civic rank; local warfare can also be enabled without
City lifecycle after adopting Living village and its prerequisites.

At Town, the optional material-preparation store can supply sets for equipment
replacement and post-battle repair. Provision service reduces actual spoilage;
at full storage its savings can overflow. Civic duty (priority 6), service (7),
practice/additional watch (4), food (1) and care (2) share finite adults. Release
extra watch or pause optional paid work when civic staffing or care needs hands.
