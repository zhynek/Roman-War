## Scope decisions

Town remains the highest civic rank. Paid screens and recurring kit repair,
care, mobilization and supply losses provide bounded local uses for resources;
this is not Large-town population or service development. Large town (G-1), the
versioned settlement-development report (G-5), recruitment and all parent
military bridges remain unimplemented. A future report could expose verified
local readiness, equipment condition, participation and recovery alongside civic
capability; it must not merge saves, transfer battle authority or imply conquest.

Warfare requires Living village (and its prerequisite household/asset/land
chapters), not City lifecycle. Lifecycle adoption remains optional. Town
facilities improve the existing production path when their conditions are met;
no new historical technology or free residents are implied.
